How to use it

The guide finds the gap. The cards close it.

1

Fifteen checks

The one-page guide asks fifteen questions about the organisation. The "not yet"s name your thinnest section.

2

Pull those cards

Each card: why it goes wrong, the move, the line. Every move ends in a named owner, a piece of evidence and a review date.

3

Pin the desk card

The whole deck on one A4 — the core five, the test, and default-up. It stays after the cards are memorised.

The printed deck

Also a boxed deck for the boardroom.

Built for the executive table

The deck runs as a governance working session: deal the sections across the table, place your live use cases on the tiers, argue the red lines until they are written, and leave with names and dates against each one.

Card faces stay typographic on purpose. Art lives on the box, the back and the wall — the accountability stays yours.
