The Governance Deck · Desk card

The tools change. Accountability does not.

The whole deck for executives and boards. Pin it up; use it before you approve, paste or escalate.

01The Line

Accountability and the gate.

The principle: every AI-assisted decision has a named human who owns the evidence and the consequence · The four questions: what does it do, what does it see, who checks it, what if wrong · The unpromptable: real-time context, calls against the data, unlistable variables · The gate: a blank field is a no.

02Oversight

Human authority and its evidence.

Tiers 1–4: autonomous (bounded) · on the loop (veto) · in the loop (decides on primary evidence) · in command (no AI origination) · Start at Tier 3; down is earned, up is default · one high on consequence, reversibility or time escalates · Evidence by tier or the tier is not claimed · Red lines written, owned, published, reviewed annually · Decision rights: one name per row · two-person rule where stakes demand.

03Data

Classification, sovereignty, the paste.

Four classes: Very High (sovereign or none) · High (approved tenant, redact) · Medium (enterprise, no training) · Low (any approved tool) · Default: cannot classify, cannot paste; mixed takes the highest class · Paste test: class and marker · where it runs · who sees it · kept or used · metadata · Sovereignty: who can compel access, not just where it sits · Sandbox: a ban is not a control; it is a hope.

04Vendors

Diligence before and after the signature.

Ten questions before the demo — training data, retention and deletion, sub-processors, human review, incidents, model change, audit rights, exit · TEVV: evidence, not badges · Model swap: notice, re-validation, or the vendor owns your operating model · Exit signed with the entry · Registry: where it runs, which model, last review.

05Learning

Registers, reviews, the loop.

One register: AI risks join the register you already run — one owner each · Brief-back before every go-live: listen for gaps, not polish · AAR monthly, blameless, near misses first.

Sampling and drift: trust decays on a schedule; sample against it · Gates at 30, 60, 90 — or the plan is a poster · Review dates are controls: every artefact carries one, and missed reviews surface by name.

The core five

The four questions · the 4-tier spectrum · red lines · the paste test · the accountability test. If you keep only five tools, keep these.

The test

Every tool and artefact must produce a named owner, a piece of evidence and a review date. If not, it is decoration.

Default up

Start at Tier 3. Move down only with evidence. Move up whenever uncertain. One high on the escalation test is enough.
