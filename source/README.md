# The Governance Deck — source note

All content distilled from the **generic frameworks** of the Leading AI with
Confidence course (Curtin Executive Education, designed and facilitated by
Dr Michael Borck):

- `curtin/exec-ed/ers/site/frameworks.md` — accountability principle, four
  questions, 4-tier oversight spectrum, tier assignment and evidence, cognitive
  hazards, red line inventory, decision rights, risk register, registry,
  ISO 42001 and NIST mappings, data classification, paste test, sovereignty
  rule, three scales, sandbox, vendor diligence, TEVV, brief-back, AAR,
  90-day plan, disclosure, use-case gate.
- `curtin/exec-ed/ers/handouts/` — desk card and field guide (format precedents).

**NDA boundary.** The course README is explicit: the generic frameworks are
Michael's IP; ERS-tailored material (ERS names, the EOC and bid scenarios,
Defence sites, role titles) is derived information under the confidentiality
agreement. This deck uses the generic frameworks only. No ERS document IDs,
scenario specifics, or role names appear on any card. The generic role labels
in the source (AI Committee, Technologies Team, HSEQ) were generalised further
to "who owns the row".

Voice: executive, declarative, accountability-first. Every card ends in an
owner, a piece of evidence, or a review date.
